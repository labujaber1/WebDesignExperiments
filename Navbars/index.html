Do we really need a footer nowadays?

Navbar's have been placed in different positions by devs with intelligent designs but the footer has mainly remained at the bottom containing predictable info. It's content may be useful but ask yourself how often will each user repeatedly visit each policy or address compared to other pages in the typical navbar. So why take up valuable page space if the data can be placed within the navbar without crowding it. Popups, modals, page links, separate page, hiding non links (badges) on smaller screens, and existing creative solutions can be used.
