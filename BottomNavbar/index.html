Navbar's have been placed in different positions and have intelligent designs but footer has mainly remained at the bottom containing predictable info. It may be usefull lightly but ask yourself how often will each user repeatedly visit each policies compared to the navbar. So why take up valuable page space if it can be placed within the navbar. Popups, modals, page links, separate page, and existing creative solutions can be used. Ask before finding a place if it is actually needed or valuable to the sites core message and business objectives.
